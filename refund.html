Refund Policy — CapAIGC Security Review

Last updated: 2026-11-12

Before work begins

If you cancel after paying but before we have started the review, you receive a full refund.

Clean-report promise

If your delivered report contains no Critical and no High findings, you may choose either: - a full refund of the checkup fee, or - a full credit of the fee toward a future engagement. Tell us within 14 days of delivery which you prefer.

After delivery

Once a report with Critical or High findings has been delivered, the fee is earned and is not refundable — the findings, evidence, and fix guidance are the product. This does not affect the clean-report promise above.

If we cannot complete the work

If we are unable to perform or complete a paid review (for example, the stack turns out to be outside the agreed scope, or an authorization problem emerges that is not your fault), you receive a full refund for any work not performed. If an authorization problem is your responsibility (section 3 of the Terms), fees for work already performed are not refundable.

Fix engagements

If you engage us to fix findings after a checkup, the checkup fee is fully credited toward the fix engagement fee, as stated at purchase.

How refunds are issued

Refunds are issued to the original payment method through our payment provider. Processing times depend on the provider and your bank.
