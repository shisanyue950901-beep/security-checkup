Inspection Scope & Deliverables

What we check

We run a structured diligence checklist across the three areas where small SaaS deals stall or re-trade — before a buyer's analyst finds them first.

01

Revenue structure

Test whether claimed MRR actually reconciles as recurring revenue.

  • Decompose subscription vs. one-time revenue, refunds, and churn.
  • Inspect customer concentration and public pricing tiers.
  • Reconcile subscriber count × ARPU against claimed MRR.
02

Growth authenticity

Cross-check your growth story against independent public timelines.

  • Compare listing claims against Web Archive and domain RDAP history.
  • Review public traffic signals and review/install trajectories.
  • Prove solid numbers — or catch gaps before a buyer rejects your offer.
03

Technical surface

Surface publicly visible technical risks that kill deals late.

  • Check public repositories and bundles for exposed keys and stale dependencies.
  • Review tenant-isolation signals, domain setup, and certificate basics.
  • Flag handover risks early while fixes remain fast and inexpensive.

Evidence Grade Legend (L0–L4)

Public data never reaches L4 on its own.
  • L0 Seller or broker assertion only. No citable source — quoted, never relied on.
  • L1 Single public observation. One live page or API response, independently re-runnable.
  • L2 Public timeline or cross-check. Two or more independent sources that agree or conflict over time.
  • L3 Reproducible technical verification. A direct probe returned a definitive status, record, or bundle response.
  • L4 Reconciled primary data. Seller-provided export reconciled against public observations — the only grade carrying a financial conclusion.

Layer labels: Every entry is also tagged [public observation], [inference] (with explicit assumptions), or [seller to provide].
