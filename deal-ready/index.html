FAQ

Questions, answered

What do you check in a Deal-Ready Report?

We run a four-level check: L0–L3 Technical Reality, L4 Financial Reality, Valuation & Market Reality, and Claims. The verdict is RAG — Red / Amber / Green — with evidence and next steps. The full Seller and Buyer Reports cover all eight sections in depth; the Quick Check is a 3-page checklist covering the same four levels at a high level.

When does the delivery clock start?

The delivery clock starts only when we have received all three: (1) your USDT payment, (2) your signed authorization, and (3) access to the code and materials we need. From that point, Quick Check is delivered within 48 hours and full Seller and Buyer Reports within 5 business days. Payment alone does not start the clock.

How do I check Founding slot availability?

The Seller Report is $299 at the Founding price. Email us first to check whether a Founding slot is available before you pay — paying does not lock a slot. Use the “Check Founding slot availability” link in the Pay with USDT section, or email shisanyue950901@gmail.com.

What do you need from me?

Your listing link is enough to start. We may ask for a read-only code walkthrough, subscription/payment records, and seller claims — whatever is needed to verify the deal. The delivery clock starts only when payment, the signed authorization, and access are all received.

What happens if the report is Red?

A Red verdict means the deal has material risks — the report explains what they are and what would need to change. You can use it to renegotiate, ask the seller to fix the issues, or walk away with evidence.
